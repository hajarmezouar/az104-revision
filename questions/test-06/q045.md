## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You configure a Recovery Services vault backup policy named Policy1 with the following settings:
Based on the information provided, select the answer choice that completes the following statement correctly.
“The backup that occurs on Sunday, March 1, will be retained for ___________.”
Réponses:
☐ 30 days
☐ 10 weeks
☐ 36 months
**☑** 10 years
Explication
`10 years` is the correct answer because the backup policy specifies retention of yearly backup points. Since the backup occurring on Sunday, March 1, aligns with the yearly retention schedule (March 1), it will be retained for the maximum period defined under the yearly backup retention, which is 10 years.
`30 days` is incorrect because this retention applies only to daily backup points, not yearly backups.
`10 weeks` is incorrect because this retention applies to weekly backup points, not yearly backups.
`36 months` is incorrect because this retention applies to monthly backup points, not yearly backups.
