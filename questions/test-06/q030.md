## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an on-premises network and an Azure subscription that includes three peered virtual networks named VNET1, VNET2, and VNET3. All three virtual networks are connected to the on-premises network.
The subscription contains the following virtual machines:
- VM1 and VM2, located in West US and connected to VNET1
- VM3, located in West US and connected to VNET2
- VM4, located in Central US and connected to VNET3
You want to monitor network connectivity between the virtual machines and the on-premises network by using Connection Monitor.
What is the minimum number of Connection Monitor instances that you must deploy?
Réponses:
☐ 1
**☑** 2
☐ 3
☐ 4
Explication
`2` is correct because Azure Network Watcher's Connection Monitor requires that the source virtual machine and the Connection Monitor instance reside in the same Azure region. In the table:
- VM1, VM2, and VM3 are in the West US region.
- VM4 is in the Central US region.
You will need:
- One Connection Monitor in West US to monitor VM1, VM2, and VM3's connectivity to the on-premises network.
- One Connection Monitor in Central US to monitor VM4’s connectivity to the on-premises network.
You cannot use a single connection monitor to monitor VMs across regions, hence two are required.
Reference(s):
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/network-watcher/connection-monitor-overview
