## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You configure a Recovery Services vault backup policy named Policy1 with the following settings:
Based on the information provided, select the answer choice that completes the following statement correctly.
“The backup that occurs on Sunday, November 1, will be retained for _____________.”
Réponses:
☐ 30 days
☐ 10 weeks
**☑** 36 months
☐ 10 years
Explication
`36 months` is the correct answer because the backup that occurs on Sunday, November 1, aligns with both the weekly and monthly retention schedules. Given that the monthly backup point retention is set to 36 months and the weekly retention is 10 weeks, Azure’s retention policy dictates that the backup will be retained for the longest period in case of overlap. Therefore, this backup will be retained for 36 months.
`10 weeks` is incorrect because, although the backup aligns with the weekly retention schedule, the 36-month retention period for the monthly backup point takes precedence due to Azure’s policy of selecting the longest retention period.
`10 years` is incorrect because yearly retention is not applicable for this backup, as it is not taken on March 1.
`30 days` is incorrect because this applies only to daily backup points, which are irrelevant for this specific backup.
