## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription called Sub1 that includes the resource groups, as shown in the table below.
Each resource group is associated with a specific Azure region and has an Azure Policy assigned to it.
A web app named CustomerWeb01 exists in ResGrp1 and is deployed in the West Europe region.
You move CustomerWeb01 to ResGrp2.
What happens as a result of this move?
Réponses:
☐ The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 moves to North Europe. Pol2 applies to CustomerWeb01.
☐ The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 remains in West Europe. Pol1 applies to CustomerWeb01.
**☑** The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 remains in West Europe. Pol2 applies to CustomerWeb01.
☐ The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 moves to North Europe. Pol1 applies to CustomerWeb01.
Explication
`The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 remains in West Europe. Pol2 applies to CustomerWeb01` is the correct answer here. When you move a resource like a web app to a different resource group, the region of the resource does not change—it stays in the same physical location unless explicitly redeployed. Therefore, CustomerWeb01 and its App Service plan will remain in West Europe.
However, after the move, the policy assigned to the destination resource group (ResGrp2) will now govern CustomerWeb01. Since CustomerWeb01 is now in ResGrp2, Pol2 applies to it.
`The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 moves to North Europe. Pol2 applies to CustomerWeb01` is incorrect because the App Service plan does not change regions automatically during a resource group move.
`The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 remains in West Europe. Pol1 applies to CustomerWeb01` is incorrect because although the location remains unchanged, the applied policy comes from the new resource group (ResGrp2), not the original one.
`The App Service plan for CustomerWeb01 moves to North Europe. Pol1 applies to CustomerWeb01` is incorrect for both reasons—the region doesn’t change, and the policy from the original group doesn’t carry over.
