## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that contains the following virtual networks:
- VNet-West (Region: West Europe)
- VNet-Asia (Region: Southeast Asia)
- VNet-US (Region: South Central US)
The subscription also includes the following subnets:
- Subnet-A
- Virtual network: VNet-West
- Service endpoint: None
- Subnet-B
- Virtual network: VNet-Asia
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-C
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-D
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: None
Additionally, the following storage accounts exist:
- storagewest01
- Region: West Europe
- Kind: StorageV2
- storageus01
- Region: South Central US
- Kind: BlobStorage
- storageasia01
- Region: Southeast Asia
- Kind: StorageV2
You create a service endpoint policy named StoragePolicy01 in the South Central US region. The policy is configured to allow access to all storage accounts in the subscription.
True or False:
`“Only storagewest01 and storageus01 can be accessed from VNet-Asia.”`
Réponses:
☐ True
**☑** False
Explication
`False` is the correct answer because Subnet-B in VNet-Asia has the Microsoft.Storage service endpoint enabled, and no service endpoint policy is associated with that subnet. When a subnet has a Storage service endpoint enabled and no endpoint policy restricting access, it can connect to any Azure Storage account that allows access through service endpoints, regardless of the region.
In this scenario:
- VNet-Asia is in Southeast Asia
- Subnet-B has the Microsoft.Storage service endpoint enabled
- No service endpoint policy is applied to Subnet-B
Therefore, Subnet-B can access all storage accounts that permit service endpoint traffic, including:
- storagewest01 (West Europe)
- storageus01 (South Central US)
- storageasia01 (Southeast Asia)
The service endpoint policy StoragePolicy01 does not affect Subnet-B because it is not associated with that subnet.
The statement is incorrect because access is not limited to only storagewest01 and storageus01. The subnet can also access storageasia01.
