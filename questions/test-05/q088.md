## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that contains the following virtual networks:
- VNet-West (Region: West Europe)
- VNet-Asia (Region: Southeast Asia)
- VNet-US (Region: South Central US)
The subscription also includes the following subnets:
- Subnet-A
- Virtual network: VNet-West
- Service endpoint: None
- Subnet-B
- Virtual network: VNet-Asia
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-C
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-D
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: None
Additionally, the following storage accounts exist:
- storagewest01
- Region: West Europe
- Kind: StorageV2
- storageus01
- Region: South Central US
- Kind: BlobStorage
- storageasia01
- Region: Southeast Asia
- Kind: StorageV2
You create a service endpoint policy named StoragePolicy01 in the South Central US region. The policy is configured to allow access to all storage accounts in the subscription.
True or False:
`"Only storageus01 can be accessed from VNet-US."`
Réponses:
☐ True
**☑** False
Explication
`False` is the correct answer because VNet-US is in the South Central US region, and both Subnet-C and Subnet-D belong to VNet-US. While Subnet-C has a Microsoft.Storage service endpoint and StoragePolicy01 applied, Subnet-D does not have any service endpoint or policy.
Here's what that means:
- Subnet-C can access only storage accounts allowed by StoragePolicy01, which includes storagewest01, storageus01, and storageasia01 (all storage accounts in the subscription), even though it is in South Central US.
- Subnet-D, which has no service endpoint configured, can still access public endpoints of any storage account over the internet if firewall rules and access configurations allow it.
Therefore, it is incorrect to say that only storageus01 can be accessed from VNet-US. In fact, storagewest01, storageus01, and storageasia01 can all potentially be accessed depending on which subnet the traffic originates from and whether it goes through the service endpoint or over the public internet.
