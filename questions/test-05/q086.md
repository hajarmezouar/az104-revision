## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that contains the following virtual networks:
- VNet-West (Region: West Europe)
- VNet-Asia (Region: Southeast Asia)
- VNet-US (Region: South Central US)
The subscription also includes the following subnets:
- Subnet-A
- Virtual network: VNet-West
- Service endpoint: None
- Subnet-B
- Virtual network: VNet-Asia
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-C
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: Microsoft.Storage
- Subnet-D
- Virtual network: VNet-US
- Service endpoint: None
Additionally, the following storage accounts exist:
- storagewest01
- Region: West Europe
- Kind: StorageV2
- storageus01
- Region: South Central US
- Kind: BlobStorage
- storageasia01
- Region: Southeast Asia
- Kind: StorageV2
You create a service endpoint policy named StoragePolicy01 in the South Central US region. The policy is configured to allow access to all storage accounts in the subscription.
True or False:
`“StoragePolicy01 can be applied to Subnet-C.”`
Réponses:
**☑** True
☐ False
Explication
`True` is the correct answer because `Subnet-C` is in VNet-US, which is located in the South Central US region, the same region in which the service endpoint policy (StoragePolicy01) was created.
For a service endpoint policy to be applied to a subnet:
- The subnet must have a matching service endpoint (in this case, `Microsoft.Storage`).
- The subnet and the service endpoint policy must be in the same region.
Since Subnet3 has a `Microsoft.Storage` endpoint enabled and is located in the South Central US region (same as StoragePolicy01), it meets both conditions and therefore Policy1 can be applied to Subnet3.
