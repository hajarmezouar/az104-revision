## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure Storage account named storage1 that has the Blob service properties shown in the following exhibit.
On January 1, the following blobs are uploaded to storage1:
- File1 is stored in the Hot access tier
- File2 is stored in the Cool access tier
- File3 is stored in the Archive access tier
You then perform these actions:
- On January 5, File1 is deleted
- On January 15, File2 is moved to the Archive access tier
- On April 5, File3 is rehydrated
True or False:
`“You are charged an early deletion penalty for File2.”`
Réponses:
**☑** True
☐ False
Explication
`True` is the correct answer here. File2 was added on January 1 with the Cool access tier and was moved to the Archive access tier on January 15; just 14 days after it was created. The Cool tier has a minimum retention period of 30 days, meaning that if a blob is deleted, overwritten, or moved to another tier before 30 days, an early deletion penalty applies.
Since File2 was moved from Cool to Archive after 14 days, Azure will apply a prorated early deletion charge for 16 days (i.e., 30 - 14). This charge reflects the unused portion of the required minimum retention period in the Cool tier.
