## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that includes an Azure Storage account named storage1.
The subscription has the following users and group memberships:
- User1 is a member of Group1.
- User2 is a member of Group2.
- User3 is a member of Group1.
You plan to monitor storage1 and configure email notifications for specific monitoring signals.
The notification requirements are as follows:
How many Action groups are required to meet all the monitoring and notification requirements.
Réponses:
☐ 1
☐ 2
**☑** 3
☐ 4
Explication
`3` is correct. An Action Group defines who gets notified and how. You need a separate Action Group only when the set of recipients (users) is different. Looking at the notification requirements:
- Ingress: Notifies User1 and User3 - Action Group 1
- Egress: Notifies User1 only - Action Group 2
- Delete storage account: Notifies User1, User2, and User3 - Action Group 3
- Restore blob ranges: Notifies User1 and User3 - Same as Ingress - reuse Action Group 1
So, only three distinct sets of recipients, hence 3 Action Groups are required.

- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/alerts/action-groups
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-monitor/alerts/alerts-overview
