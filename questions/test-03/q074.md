## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure Storage account named storage1 that has the Blob service properties shown in the following exhibit.
On January 1, the following blobs are uploaded to storage1:
- File1 is stored in the Hot access tier
- File2 is stored in the Cool access tier
- File3 is stored in the Archive access tier
You then perform these actions:
- On January 5, File1 is deleted
- On January 15, File2 is moved to the Archive access tier
- On April 5, File3 is rehydrated
True or False:
`“You are charged an early deletion penalty for File3.”`
Réponses:
**☑** True
☐ False
Explication
`True` is the correct answer here. File3 was uploaded on January 1 and placed in the Archive access tier. It was then rehydrated (i.e., restored to an online tier such as Hot or Cool) on April 5, which is 94 days after it was archived.
The Archive tier has a minimum retention period of 180 days. If a blob is rehydrated, deleted, or moved before 180 days, Azure applies a prorated early deletion fee for the remaining days.
In this case, File3 remained in the Archive tier for only 94 days, so an early deletion charge equivalent to 86 days (180 - 94) will be incurred.
Reference(s.):
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-overview#early-deletion-charge
