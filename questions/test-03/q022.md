## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that includes several virtual networks deployed across different regions and resource groups.
The virtual networks are configured as follows:
- VNET1 is deployed in the West US region and belongs to resource group RG1.
- VNET2 is deployed in the Central US region and belongs to resource group RG1.
- VNET3 is deployed in the Central US region and belongs to resource group RG2.
- VNET4 is deployed in the West US region and belongs to resource group RG2.
You plan to deploy an Azure Firewall named AF1 in resource group RG1, located in the West US region.
Which virtual networks are valid targets for deploying AF1?
Réponses:
**☑** VNET1 only
☐ VNET1 and VNET2 only
☐ VNET1 and VNET4 only
☐ VNET1, VNET2, and VNET4 only
☐ VNET1, VNET2, VNET3, and VNET4
Explication
`VNET1 only` is correct because Azure Firewall can connect to a virtual network within the same region and resource group. In this scenario, AF1 is deployed in RG1 in the West US region. While VNET4 is also in the West US region, it is in a different resource group (RG2), which prevents AF1 from connecting to it. Therefore, VNET1 is the only virtual network eligible for connection to AF1.
