## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure Storage account named storage1 that has the Blob service properties shown in the following exhibit.
On January 1, the following blobs are uploaded to storage1:
- File1 is stored in the Hot access tier
- File2 is stored in the Cool access tier
- File3 is stored in the Archive access tier
You then perform these actions:
- On January 5, File1 is deleted
- On January 15, File2 is moved to the Archive access tier
- On April 5, File3 is rehydrated
True or False:
`“You are charged an early deletion penalty for File1.”`
Réponses:
☐ True
**☑** False
Explication
`False` is the correct answer here. File1 was uploaded to the storage account under the Hot access tier on January 1 and then deleted on January 5. The Hot tier does not have an early deletion penalty, regardless of the deletion time.
For reference, the access tiers and their minimum retention periods for avoiding early deletion charges are as follows:
- Hot: No minimum retention period; early deletion penalty does not apply.
- Cool: 30 days minimum retention period.
- Cold: 90 days minimum retention period.
- Archive: 180 days minimum retention period.
Since File1 was stored in the Hot tier, it incurs no early deletion penalty even if it is deleted within days of upload.
Reference(s.):
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-overview
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/access-tiers-overview#early-deletion-charge
