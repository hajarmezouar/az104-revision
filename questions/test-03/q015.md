## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


Note: This question is part of a series that uses the same overall scenario. Each question in the series proposes a different solution that may or may not satisfy the stated requirement.
You have an Azure subscription that contains 10 virtual networks, each deployed in its own resource group.
Another administrator intends to create multiple network security groups (NSGs) within the subscription.
You must ensure that every newly created NSG automatically blocks TCP traffic on port 8080 between the virtual networks.
Proposed solution: Assign a built-in Azure Policy definition at the subscription scope.
Does this solution satisfy the requirement?
Réponses:
☐ Yes
**☑** No
Explication
`No` is the correct answer here. To meet the specific requirement of automatically blocking TCP port 8080 between virtual networks when an NSG is created, you would need to create a custom Azure policy. Built-in policies provided by Microsoft focus on general compliance and best practices and do not cover specific, custom configurations such as blocking a specific port across all new NSGs. Creating a custom policy allows you to define specific actions, such as blocking or allowing certain ports, tailored to your Azure environment's unique needs.
Reference(s):
- https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-policy/policy-definition
- https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/governance/policy/tutorials/create-custom-policy-definition
