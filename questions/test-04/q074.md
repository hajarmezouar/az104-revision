## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have a pay-as-you-go Azure subscription with the following virtual machines:
- VM1 in resource group RG1, costing 20 euros per day
- VM2 in resource group RG2, costing **30 euros per day
You create the budget shown in the following exhibit.
The budget is set up with alert thresholds at 50%, 70%, and 100%, each linked to different action groups.
The action group AG1 contains only the user admin@apexcore.com.
Based on this configuration, complete the following statement:
“When VM1 and VM2 reach the maximum budget limit, ___________________________________.”
Réponses:
☐ Both VM1 and VM2 are automatically shut down.
**☑** VM1 and VM2 continue running.
☐ VM1 is stopped, while VM2 continues to run.
Explication
`VM1 and VM2 continue running` is the correct answer here. Azure budgets are a monitoring and alerting tool only. They do not enforce or take automatic action such as shutting down resources. In the case study:
- Budget1 is scoped to Resource Group RG1, which contains VM1.
- VM2, which is in RG2, is not within the scope of the budget and is therefore not tracked or managed by this budget.
- Even when the budget is fully consumed (100%), the configured action groups (AG1, AG2, AG3) only send notifications (via Email, SMS, or Azure app), but do not stop or deallocate any virtual machines.
Therefore, when both VM1 and VM2 reach their maximum cost, they continue to run, and no automatic shutdown occurs.
