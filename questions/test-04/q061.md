## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have an Azure subscription that includes the storage accounts listed below:
- storage1: StorageV2 with geo-zone-redundant storage (GZRS)
- storage2: BlobStorage with read-access geo-redundant storage (RA-GRS)
- storage3: BlockBlobStorage with zone-redundant storage (ZRS)
You need to determine which of these storage accounts support Azure Blob lifecycle management policies.
Which storage accounts meet this requirement?
Réponses:
☐ storage1 only
☐ storage2 only
☐ storage1 and storage3 only
☐ storage2 and storage3 only
**☑** storage1, storage2, and storage3
Explication
`storage1, storage2, and storage3` is correct because all three storage account types—StorageV2, BlobStorage, and BlockBlobStorage—support lifecycle management policies for block blobs and append blobs. These policies enable automated tiering, deletion, and management of blob data to optimize costs and resource usage.
Reference(s):
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/storage/blobs/lifecycle-management-overview
