## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You have three Azure storage accounts configured as follows:
- storage1 — General-purpose v2 (StorageV2), Standard performance, Locally-redundant storage (LRS), Cool tier
- storage2 — General-purpose v2 (StorageV2), Standard performance, Read-access geo-redundant storage (RA-GRS), Hot tier
- storage3 — BlobStorage, Premium performance, Locally-redundant storage (LRS), Hot tier
You must identify which storage account can be changed to use zone-redundant storage (ZRS) replication.
Which storage account meets this requirement?
Réponses:
**☑** storage1 only
☐ storage2 only
☐ storage3 only
☐ storage2 and storage3
☐ storage1, storage2, and storage3
Explication
`storage1 only` is correct because it is an LRS account that can directly be converted to a ZRS account.
`storage2 only` is incorrect because RA-GRS account cannot be directly converted to ZRS. However, it can be converted to LRS and then finally converted to ZRS. So technically, both Storage1 and Storage2 accounts can be converted to ZRS, but since we do not have "Storage1 and Storage2 only" as an option in the question, we are assuming it to be asking about direct conversion only.
`storage3` is incorrect because BlobStorage accounts are not eligible for ZRS replication, regardless of their performance or replication settings.
`storage2 and storage3 only` is  incorrect answer here. Storage2 account is RA-GRS which cannot be converted to ZRS directly. Storage3  being a BlobStorage account is not supported for ZRS replication.
`storage1, storage2, and storage3` are incorrect because only storage1 meets the criteria for direct conversion to ZRS.
