## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You manage a Microsoft Entra tenant.
You need to create a Microsoft 365 group that dynamically includes only users who belong to the finance department and are located in US.
To accomplish this, you must complete the dynamic membership rule shown below. The rule evaluates a user attribute for department membership and combines it with a country-based condition.
What should you choose for `Code Segment A`?
Réponses:
☐ device.managementType
☐ device.organizationalUnit
**☑** user.department
☐ user.usageLocation
Explication
`user.department` is correct because the requirement is to create a group that contains members from a specific department, in this case, the finance department. The user.department attribute in dynamic membership rules is used to filter users based on their department property.
