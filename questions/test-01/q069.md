]You then add 14 virtual machines to WEBPROD-AS-USE2.
Select the answer choice that completes the following statement based on the information presented.
`“When Microsoft carries out planned maintenance in the East US 2 region, the highest number of virtual machines that could be unavailable at the same time will be __________.”`
Réponses:
**☑** 2
☐ 7
☐ 10
☐ 14
Explication
`2` is the correct answer here. The Availability Set named WEBPROD-AS-USE2 is configured with 10 update domains, as shown in the exhibit (`"platformUpdateDomainCount": 10`). When Azure performs planned maintenance, only one update domain is rebooted at a time to maintain high availability.
If there are 14 virtual machines in the availability set, Azure distributes them evenly across the 10 update domains. This means:
- 4 update domains will host 2 VMs each.
- 6 update domains will host 1 VM each.
Since maintenance affects only one update domain at a time, the maximum number of VMs that can be offline simultaneously is 2—the worst case being an update domain with 2 VMs.
This behavior ensures that at least 12 out of 14 VMs remain available during any planned maintenance event.
