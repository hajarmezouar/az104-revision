## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


You manage a Microsoft Entra tenant.
You need to create a Microsoft 365 group that dynamically includes only users who belong to the finance department and are located in US.
To accomplish this, you must complete the dynamic membership rule shown below. The rule evaluates a user attribute for department membership and combines it with a country-based condition.
What should you choose for `Code Segment B`?
Réponses:
**☑** and
☐ or
☐ typeof
Explication
`and` is correct because you want to include users who meet both criteria: being in the finance department and being located in US. Using "and" ensures that both conditions must be true for a user to be included in the dynamic membership.
