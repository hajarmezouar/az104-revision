## Énoncé et corrigé fourni


An Azure subscription includes a storage account named storageaccount1. The storage account has the following lifecycle management rules configured:
On June 1, you upload the following blobs to storageaccount1:
- File1 is stored in container1 and uses the Hot access tier.
- File2 is stored in container2 and uses the Hot access tier.
True or False:**
`"On June 16, File2 will be stored in the Archive access tier."`
Réponses:
☐ True
**☑** False
Explication
`False` is correct because after 10 days, rule3 deletes the file2 and hence it will not be available to be put to archive tier after 15 days by rule4.
